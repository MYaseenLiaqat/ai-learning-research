# AI-Assisted Learning Study

## 1. Research Question

Does AI assistance moderate the relationship between immediate
unaided performance and subsequent retention and transfer
in programming learning?

## 2. Primary Hypothesis

The relationship between immediate independent performance
and later retention/transfer differs between learners who
receive AI assistance and learners who do not.

## 3. Domain

Python programming.

## 4. Experimental Conditions

### Condition A — No AI

Learners complete the learning activity without generative AI.

### Condition B — Controlled AI

Learners receive generative AI assistance under a fixed,
standardized protocol.

## 5. Outcomes

- Immediate independent performance
- Delayed retention
- Transfer
- Future unaided criterion performance

## 6. Primary Statistical Test

AI × Immediate Performance interaction predicting:

- delayed retention
- transfer

## 7. Important Limitation

Randomized AI exposure improves causal identification but
reduces ecological validity compared with naturally occurring
AI use.
