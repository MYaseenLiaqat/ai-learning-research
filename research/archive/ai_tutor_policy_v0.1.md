# Controlled AI Tutor Policy v0.1

## Status
**Historical reconstruction.**

Reconstructed on 2026-09-08 from system prompt version `0.1.0`. This standalone policy document did not exist contemporaneously.

## Associated system prompt
`0.1.0`

## Policy
The initial controlled tutor:
- used the same policy for every participant;
- was instructed to give concise conceptual guidance and small hints;
- was instructed not to reveal hidden tests;
- was instructed not to invent tasks;
- was instructed not to personalize the experimental treatment.

## Limitations
The prompt did not yet explicitly define:
- the narrow Loops construct boundary;
- forbidden constructs;
- cross-task-memory restrictions;
- the platform-provided-input contract;
- a rule governing complete-solution requests;
- research-hypothesis disclosure.

These controls were added/tightened later.
